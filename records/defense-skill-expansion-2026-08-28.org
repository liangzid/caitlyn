#+title: Defense Skill Library Expansion
#+date: 2026-08-28

* Objective
- Audit whether the shipped antibody library contains substantive and executable defenses.
- Search recent defense research and add non-duplicate methods that fit the CAITLYN defense-skill model.

* Current library findings
- The shipped library contains 24 entries: 14 detectors and 10 non-detectors.
- Tier 0 has 10 scripted detectors and 2 signature-only detectors.
- Only 2 Tier 1 entries execute as independent detectors.
- Eight non-detectors contribute prompt knowledge only in merged execution.
- Two Tier 0 non-detectors are not reachable from the scanner.
- All 24 shipped antibody directories lack the README required by the documented skill format.

* Research scope
- Established 2024--2025 candidates: Task Shield, CaMeL, IPIGuard,
  IsolateGPT, DataSentinel, StruQ, and SecAlign.
- Experimental 2026 candidates: SARA, ToolMinimize, AgentFlow,
  TrustShiftProbe, TraceGrant, TRUSS, CompoSkill, and SkillsMetric.
- Existing implementations such as Spotlighting, Instruction Hierarchy,
  and MELON must not be duplicated.

* Agreed direction
- 团长 approved collecting reasonable methods from both periods.
- New 2026 methods must be marked experimental until independently validated.
- A method must not be represented as executable when CAITLYN lacks the
  context, model, runtime hook, or policy mechanism required by the paper.

* Result
- Added 15 source-linked research entries, comprising 7 works from 2024--2025
  and 8 works from 2026.
- Added implementation_status, execution_stages, references, and
  runtime_requirements to the library schema.
- Runtime selectors execute only active entries. Experimental and reference
  entries are disabled by default.
- Documented all 24 pre-existing entries and rebuilt the 39-entry index.
- Verified all 15 source URLs, the TypeScript build, the library audit, all 10
  active Tier 0 scripts, and 434 automated tests.
