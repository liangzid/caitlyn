* PR=1.0 is not a formula bug (2026-08-20)

团长 questioned why many methods sit at precision 1.0. Operating-point
precision is TP/(TP+FP) with FP=0 on the 250 shared benign pool
(Table 2 FPR=0). LLM-Judge/PI/Regex fire 0/250. CAITLYN fires 8/250,
precision 0.90-0.97. The pool is AgentDefense-Bench/XSTest-style short
prompts (kill a Python process, blow up a balloon), not tool-output
lookalikes. Changing the ranking score would not lower that plateau.
Changing the PR formula would be fabricating FPs.
