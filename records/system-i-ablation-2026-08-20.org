* Read for 4.4

  SafeClaw. Do not attribute the 5.2 vs 3.6 FPR gap to T0: t0-only FPR
  is 0, so every benign string still reaches merged-pair.

- Single merge is cheaper/cleaner (FPR 1.2) but drops SafeClaw to 55.4.
- Full (merged-pair) sits between ensemble and single merge: recall near
  ensemble on AgentDojo, FPR 3.6 vs ensemble 8.4.
